About the Foundation

Moses Peace Foundation exists to restore wholeness, dignity, and hope to young girls and women. We are committed to creating safe spaces of healing, love, and empowerment, so every woman can rise into her full, peaceful self.
2 Corinthians 4:8-10

Vision, Mission & Objectives

Vision

A world where every wounded girl and woman becomes whole — healed, seen, and empowered to thrive.

Mission

We create safe spaces of healing, love, and empowerment, restoring wholeness, dignity, and hope to girls and women.

Objectives

  • Holistic programs for healing & faith
  • Mentorship circles & safe havens
  • Events, retreats & workshops
  • Outreach & advocacy
  • Partnerships with schools & communities
  • Storytelling & creative platforms
